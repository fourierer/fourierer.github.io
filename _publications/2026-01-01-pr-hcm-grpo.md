We proposed HCM-GRPO, a self-developed training method, to train compact multimodal models (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-3B) with SFT for spatial plausibility reasoning and screening of AIGC-generated product images. Our method achieves 70%+ overall spatial reasoning accuracy and ~80% deformation reasoning accuracy, and outperforms larger baselines. Corresponding author and co-first author.
