I am an algorithm engineer at **Alibaba Health**, working on computer vision and multimodal understanding for e-commerce and medical scenarios. My current work focuses on:

- **OCR for medical scenarios**: text-line detection and recognition for medical PDF books, drug instructions, and product images (DBNet + TrOCR), achieving commercial-grade accuracy.
- **Multimodal LLM information extraction**: attribute extraction for e-commerce using SFT and self-developed DPA-GRPO on InternVL3-38B.
- **AIGC and spatial reasoning**: spatial plausibility reasoning and screening of AIGC-generated product images with compact multimodal models (Qwen2.5-VL-3B) trained with SFT and HCM-GRPO.

Before joining industry, I received my M.S. in Computer Technology from the **Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences** (2019–2022), where I worked on animal pose estimation and action recognition, building the Caged Monkey dataset with 6,000+ images and 600+ videos. I also interned at **Microsoft Research Asia**, where I proposed TSRFormer, a transformer-based method for table structure recognition.

I hold two B.S. degrees from **Tianjin University**: Mathematics and Applied Mathematics (2015–2019) and Computer Science and Technology (2016–2019, double degree).
